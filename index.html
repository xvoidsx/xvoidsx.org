01

Small by design. Ambitious by nature.

We don't want to own your attention. We want to help you own your computer — and have a good time doing it.

FOSS is not a category. It is how the future stays ours.

We make systems with a point of view: local before cloud, repair before replacement, open protocols before walled gardens, and personality over beige software.

The work is human-led and agent-accelerated. Raven writes the systems. Ryoko shapes the experience. Lain moves between code, docs, research, and the smallweb. Three operators, one shared signal.

Hardware keeps the software free.

Buying a navi machine funds continued system development, real-device testing, xvoidsx infrastructure, and support for the upstream free-software projects beneath it. navi itself remains free to install on the computer you already have.
